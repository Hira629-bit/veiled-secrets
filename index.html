Hira Butt is a Pakistani author whose love for storytelling has been shaped by a lifelong fascination with the power of words. A graduate in English Literature and Linguistics, she has always been drawn to mystery and thrillers — the kind of stories that hook a reader and don't let go.

Veiled Secrets is her debut novel, marking the start of a literary journey built on curiosity, creativity, and a passion for meaningful storytelling.
